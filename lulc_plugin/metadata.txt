about=This plugin performs Land Use/Land Cover (LULC) classification using Sentinel-2 imagery, detecting five classes: water, vegetation, built-up, bare soil, and snow. Notes on accuracy: Sentinel-2 10m spatial resolution means small or narrow features (thin streams, individual buildings) may not be fully captured. Small gaps may appear in results due to cloud masking, since heavily clouded pixels have no usable data in any composite date. Widening the date range can reduce this. Default thresholds are tuned to approximate real-world classification but are not guaranteed to be fully accurate for every study area. Adjusting thresholds for your specific area, and comparing results against the Sentinel-2 composite or Google satellite imagery from the same time period, is recommended for best results. Perfect accuracy is not guaranteed.
